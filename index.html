About Us

A Machine Shop That
Actually Talks to You

Sentinel Machining is a precision CNC machine shop in Austin, Texas. We specialize in custom aluminum and steel parts — from one-off prototypes to short production runs.

What makes us different: you talk directly to the machinist. No sales reps, no account managers, no game of telephone. Send a drawing, get a quote, ask a question — you'll hear back from the person actually making your parts.

We work with you from concept to finished part. Need to validate a design before committing to metal? We'll 3D print it first, iterate until it's right, then machine the final version.
