About Us

Sentinel Machining is a precision CNC machine shop in Austin, Texas. We specialize in custom aluminum and steel parts — from one-off prototypes to short production runs.

What makes us different: we don't just cut metal. We work with you from concept to finished part. Need help with design? We'll collaborate on it. Need to validate a prototype before committing to a full production run? We'll 3D print it first, then machine the final version in metal.

We're built for small businesses, startups, and custom fabricators who need quality parts without the overhead of a big shop. You talk directly to the machinist — no sales reps, no runaround, no six-week lead times.

Our Services

CNC Milling

Precision 3-axis CNC machining in aluminum and steel. From simple brackets to complex multi-setup parts. We work with 6061 and 7075 aluminum, mild steel, and tool steel. Tight tolerances, clean finishes, fast turnaround.

3D Printing & Prototyping

Rapid prototyping with multi-material 3D printing. Get a functional prototype in your hands in days, not weeks. Test fit, form, and function before committing to metal. Available in PLA, PETG, ABS, TPU, and more — with multi-color capability.

Design & Engineering

Got a napkin sketch? A rough idea? We'll work with you to turn it into a manufacturable part. We handle the CAD design, toolpath planning, and fixturing. You just tell us what the part needs to do. From concept to production — one shop, one point of contact.
