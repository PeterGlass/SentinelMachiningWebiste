Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

This policy explains how Sentinel Machining LLC ("Sentinel Machining," "we," "us") collects and uses information through sentinelmachining.com and in the course of running our business. We are a small CNC machining shop in Austin, Texas.

Information we collect

  • Contact form and email. When you use our contact form or email us, we receive the information you choose to send. This is usually your name, email address, phone number, company, and details or files about your project.
  • Basic technical data. Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts and styles from third-party providers. Those providers may log standard request data such as your IP address and browser type. We do not run our own analytics or advertising trackers.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to you, prepare quotes, make and deliver parts, invoice, and keep normal business records. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Service providers

We share information only with providers that help us run the business. These include our form-handling service (FormSubmit), email and document providers (Google), our accounting software, and shipping carriers. Each receives only what it needs to provide its service.

Google user data

We use a private, internal application named "Hermes Gmail" to help our own staff manage Sentinel Machining's own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public. It accesses Gmail data (and, if enabled, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Contacts) only for the account owner who authorized it. It uses that data only to read, search, organize, and draft or send messages at the account owner's direction.

Google user data is stored only on our own private server. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not transferred to third parties except as needed to provide the features above or as required by law, and not read by humans other than the account owner. The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The account owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Retention and security

We keep project and business records for as long as we need them for the job, for warranty or support, and for tax and legal purposes. We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
